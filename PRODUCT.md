# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students using a desktop study tool for self-learning. They are usually working through one math problem at a time, often switching between typed questions, formulas, and screenshots or photos of exercises.

## Product Purpose

This product helps students submit math problems to AI models and receive clear Chinese step-by-step solutions with readable LaTeX formulas. Success means the student can quickly capture a problem, understand the reasoning, and keep the answer usable for review.

## Brand Personality

Professional, quiet, focused. The interface should feel reliable and low-friction rather than playful or promotional.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style landing pages, decorative AI dashboards, flashy gradients, oversized hero layouts, and chat interfaces that hide practical study actions behind visual noise.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the problem-solving workflow direct: input, solve, review.
2. Make mathematical content readable before making the interface expressive.
3. Prefer familiar controls and predictable states over novel interactions.
4. Surface study actions close to the answer they affect.
5. Treat errors, empty states, and loading as part of the learning flow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-accessible controls. Avoid relying on color alone for critical state, keep motion minimal, and make formula-heavy content readable in a sustained study session.
